
RPL Mapping Trainer and RPL Educator Companion (& ELearn Mapping Trainer)

An open-source digital tool supporting Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) assessment in the Australian Vocational Education and Training (VET) sector.

Both products are designed to assist vocational educators and assessors to organise applicant evidence, map that evidence against units of competency, identify potential evidence gaps, and support supplementary assessment. The RPL Educator Companion is the baseline version that can be used to assess log evidence, identify gaps and single units of competence. RPL Mapping Trainer can be used for assessment but is a separate fork of the original product designed to be used as a teaching tool to assist assessors to develop their mapping skills, classify assessment evidence, and finetune gap assessment. It can be used as to simulate an RPL process and orient the process to the principles of assessment and rules of evidence.

The project has been developed by Stuart Anderson / Fastigiata Lab and is released as Free and Open Source Software under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0).
Purpose

Recognition of Prior Learning can involve substantial evidence collection, organisation, mapping and professional judgement.

RPL Mapping Trainer is intended to provide assessors with a practical digital learning space that supports this process while retaining the assessor's professional judgement and responsibility for assessment decisions.

The application is intended as an assessment support tool, not a replacement for qualified assessor judgement or an RTO's assessment and quality-assurance processes.
Dynamic Card Mapping™

A central feature of RPL Educator Companion and RPL Mapping Trainer is Dynamic Card Mapping™, an evidence-mapping approach conceived and developed by Stuart Anderson / Fastigiata Lab.

Applicant evidence is represented as a collection of digital evidence cards.

Assessors can interactively drag, drop and stack these cards against the requirements of a unit of competency, including:

    Performance Criteria
    Performance Evidence
    Knowledge Evidence
    Assessment Conditions
    other applicable competency requirements

This creates a visual representation of the relationship between the applicant's submitted evidence and the requirements of the unit.

Once evidence has been mapped, the application can assist the assessor to identify areas where the available evidence may not sufficiently address unit requirements.

Potential gaps can then inform structured supplementary questioning or the collection of additional evidence.

The final assessment decision remains the responsibility of the appropriately qualified assessor.
Key Features

RPL Educator Companion and.RPL Mapping Trainer are being developed to support:

    recording and organising applicant evidence;
    representing evidence as reusable digital evidence cards;
    visual drag-and-drop evidence mapping;
    mapping evidence across multiple competency requirements;
    stacking multiple pieces of evidence against individual requirements;
    identification of potential evidence gaps;
    structured supplementary questioning;
    recording assessment information; and
    supporting a transparent and reviewable RPL assessment process.

Features and workflows may continue to evolve as the project is tested and developed.
Open Source

RPL Educator Companion and RPL Mapping Trainer are open-source projects.

The aim is to make practical RPL technology available to vocational educators, assessors and Registered Training Organisations while enabling the broader VET community to inspect, use, adapt and improve the software.

Contributions, testing and constructive feedback from the VET and open-source communities are welcomed.
